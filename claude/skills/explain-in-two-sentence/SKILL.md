Rules:
- No preamble ("Sure, here's...", "This is...").
- No headers, no bullets, no code blocks unless a single short inline snippet is essential.
- No closing summary or follow-up offer.
- If the thing is ambiguous, pick the most likely interpretation and explain that — do not ask a clarifying question.
